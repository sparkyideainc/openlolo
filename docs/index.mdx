LoloBox connects an AI assistant to your iPhone. With your permission, the AI can see the screen, tap and type on your behalf. You choose which AI can connect and can pause phone control at any time.

## 1. Get your LoloBox

- [Buy a LoloBox](/docs/get-your-lolobox/buy-a-lolobox) — start here if you want a ready-made box.
- [Build your own](/docs/get-your-lolobox/build-one-yourself) — choose parts, assemble the box and install OpenLolo.

**Already have a box with OpenLolo installed?** Go straight to step 2.

## 2. Pair your LoloBox

[Pair your LoloBox](/docs/pair-your-lolobox) with your iPhone and connect it to home Wi-Fi. The setup page guides you through each step.

## 3. Connect your first AI

[Choose your AI and start talking](/docs/connect-your-first-ai/choose-your-ai-and-start-talking) — give your AI permission and try a first conversation.

## After your first conversation

Explore [remote access options](/docs/remote-access) when you want to change how your AI reaches the box. If you get stuck, visit [Help](/docs/troubleshoot/troubleshooting-by-symptom).
