---
title: Build your own OpenLolo
description: Turn a small ARM64 board into an OpenLolo box for your iPhone.
---

OpenLolo is a small ARM64 Linux box bound to one iPhone. AI agents connect to it over MCP and can see and operate the phone: screenshots, touch, typing, hardware buttons, and app and device information. You approve every client and can pause them at any time.

This guide takes you from parts on a desk to a working box with AI clients connected:

1. [Choose your hardware](/docs/start/choose-hardware)
2. [Build the box](/docs/build/radxa-zero-3w)
3. [Install OpenLolo](/docs/install/install-command) with one command
4. [Set up remote access](/docs/remote-access/tailscale) with Tailscale
5. [Set up the box](/docs/setup/prepare-iphone) and pair your iPhone
6. [Connect AI clients](/docs/ai-clients/connect)
