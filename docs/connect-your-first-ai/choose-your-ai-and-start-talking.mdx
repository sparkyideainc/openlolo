title: Choose your AI and start talking
description: Connect an AI assistant, approve it and try your first conversation.

Start here when your setup page says **Your box is ready**. Keep your iPhone and box connected, and open the box page on the iPhone so you can approve access.

## 1. Choose an AI

You can connect Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Claude Desktop. Start with an AI app you already use.

## 2. Add your LoloBox connection

Your AI needs a connection address for your box. It is called the **MCP URL**: MCP is the connection method the AI uses to talk to LoloBox.

For Claude.ai and ChatGPT, use the **remote MCP URL** from your box's installation details. If you built the box, the installer printed this address when remote access was set up.

If you do not have a remote address yet, [set up remote access](/docs/remote-access/set-up-tailscale) before continuing. If you deliberately use your box only on your home network, see [local access](/docs/remote-access/skip-it-or-add-it-later).

In your AI app's connection settings, add that address as an **HTTP MCP server**.

## 3. Approve access

The AI opens a consent page. Review the request and approve it on your box page. This gives that AI permission to use your iPhone for the time you choose.

## 4. Try a first conversation

Ask your AI:

> Take a screenshot of my iPhone and describe what you see.

If the AI can describe your phone's screen, the connection is working. You can now ask it to help with tasks on your iPhone.

You can stop phone control at any time with **Pause phone control** on the box page's **Home** screen. See [Control AI access](/docs/connect-your-first-ai/control-ai-access) to manage permissions.

If the connection does not work, check that the box page shows the iPhone is connected and that you approved the AI's request. Then visit [Help](/docs/troubleshoot/troubleshooting-by-symptom).
