---
title: Control AI access
description: Choose which AI can use your iPhone and stop access when you need to.
---

You stay in control of which AI can use your iPhone.

- **Approve a request:** review the AI's access request on the box page and choose how long to allow access.
- **Pause phone control:** open **Home** on the box page and tap **Pause phone control** to stop AI actions.
- **Remove an AI's access:** open **Access** on the box page and revoke its permission.

Next: return to your AI app and try a conversation. If you have not connected one yet, follow [Choose your AI and start talking](/docs/connect-your-first-ai/choose-your-ai-and-start-talking).
