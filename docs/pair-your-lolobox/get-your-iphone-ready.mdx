---
title: Get your iPhone ready
description: Check your iPhone before opening setup.
---

1. Check that your iPhone uses iOS 26 or later in **Settings → General → About**.
2. Have your passcode ready. You will need to unlock the phone and approve its connection to the box.
3. Turn on portrait orientation lock in Control Center so the screen stays upright.

During setup, the box will ask you to turn on **Developer Mode**. This is an iPhone setting that allows the box to control the phone. You can do it after connecting the USB cable; you do not need to find it before starting.

Next: [Open the setup page](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/open-the-setup-page).
