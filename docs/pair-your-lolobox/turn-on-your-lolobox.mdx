---
title: Turn on your LoloBox
description: Connect your box to power and get ready to open setup.
---

1. Connect your LoloBox to its power supply. If you built it yourself, use the power connection shown in your build guide.
2. Keep your iPhone, USB data cable and setup card nearby while the box starts.
3. On your iPhone, open **Settings → Wi-Fi** and look for the network named on your setup card.

The setup card contains the box's Wi-Fi name, password and a QR code. If you built the box, this is the card printed at the end of installation. You can [print it again](/docs/get-your-lolobox/build-one-yourself/what-the-installer-prints) if needed.
