---
title: Connect to home Wi-Fi
description: Put your LoloBox and iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network.
---

After the iPhone connection steps, the setup page asks for your home Wi-Fi.

1. Choose your home Wi-Fi name on the setup page and enter its password.
2. Wait while the box checks the connection and switches networks. The setup page may briefly lose its connection to the box.
3. On the iPhone, open **Settings → Wi-Fi** and join the same home network if it has not switched automatically.
4. Return to the setup page. It looks for the box on your home network. If needed, use the **Open** link with your box's name shown on the page.

If the password is wrong, the box reopens its setup Wi-Fi so you can reconnect and try again.

<Callout type="success" title="Your box is ready">When the setup page shows **Your box is ready**, your LoloBox and iPhone are connected. You can now connect an AI.</Callout>
