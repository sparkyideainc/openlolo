---
title: Open the setup page
description: Join your LoloBox's temporary Wi-Fi from your iPhone.
---

Your LoloBox creates a temporary Wi-Fi network for setup. It is separate from your home Wi-Fi.

1. On your iPhone, scan the QR code on the setup card and join the network. Or open **Settings → Wi-Fi**, choose the name on the card and enter its password.
2. Open the local setup page shown on the card if it does not open automatically.
3. Keep using this iPhone for setup. If the page asks you to connect the phone with a cable, do that next.

<Callout title="No internet on this network?">That is expected while you are connected to the box's temporary Wi-Fi. Stay connected to finish setup.</Callout>
