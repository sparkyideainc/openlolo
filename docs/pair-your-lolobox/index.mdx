---
title: Pair your LoloBox
description: Pair your iPhone and connect to Wi-Fi with the guided setup page.
---

Start here once OpenLolo is installed on your box. If you are building your own and have not installed it yet, [finish installation first](/docs/get-your-lolobox/build-one-yourself/first-boot).

Have your iPhone, USB data cable, setup card and home Wi-Fi password ready. Use the iPhone you want the AI to control for these steps.

1. [Turn on your box](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/turn-on-your-lolobox).
2. [Get your iPhone ready](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/get-your-iphone-ready).
3. [Open the setup page](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/open-the-setup-page) by joining the box's temporary Wi-Fi.
4. [Connect your iPhone](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/connect-your-iphone) with the USB cable, tap **Trust** and turn on Developer Mode when asked.
5. [Connect to home Wi-Fi](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/connect-to-home-wifi).

The setup page moves forward as each step finishes. You do not need to understand the technical details.

When it says **Your box is ready**, continue to [Choose your AI and start talking](/docs/connect-your-first-ai/choose-your-ai-and-start-talking).
