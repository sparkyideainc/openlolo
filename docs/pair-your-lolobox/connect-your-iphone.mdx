---
title: Connect your iPhone
description: Plug in your iPhone, approve the connection and follow the setup page.
---

Keep the setup page open on the iPhone you want to connect.

## Plug in and tap Trust

1. Connect the iPhone to the box with a USB data cable.
2. Unlock the iPhone. When **Trust This Computer?** appears, tap **Trust** and enter your passcode.
3. Keep the cable connected. The setup page moves on automatically.

This connection is called pairing. It lets the box recognize your iPhone and also prepares it to connect over Wi-Fi later.

## Turn on Developer Mode when asked

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode** on the iPhone.
2. Turn it on and tap **Restart**.
3. After the restart, unlock the phone and tap **Turn On** to confirm. Enter your passcode if asked.
4. Return to the setup page. If needed, rejoin the box's setup Wi-Fi using your setup card.

If Developer Mode is not visible, first make sure you have connected the cable and tapped **Trust**, then check Settings again.

The box handles the remaining phone preparation automatically. Keep the iPhone plugged in and unlocked, and keep the box connected to the internet while it finishes.

If you tapped **Don't Trust**, unplug the cable, plug it back in and tap **Trust** when the prompt returns. If the phone is not found, try a cable that supports data transfer.
