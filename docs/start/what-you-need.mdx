---
title: What you need
description: Phone, accessories and accounts to have ready.
---

- An iPhone on iOS 17.4 or later, with Developer Mode turned on
- A microSD card (16 GB or larger)
- A USB data cable for the iPhone (charge-only cables do not work)
- A power supply for your board
- A computer to flash the microSD card and SSH into the box
- A free [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) account, for remote access from Claude.ai and ChatGPT
