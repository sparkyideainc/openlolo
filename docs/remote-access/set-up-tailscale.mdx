---
title: Set up Tailscale
description: Approve the box in your tailnet and turn on Funnel.
---

The installer sets up Tailscale by default.

1. Open the login URL, or scan the QR code, that the installer prints. Approve the box in your tailnet.
2. On a tailnet's first use, Tailscale shows a link to turn on HTTPS and Funnel. Open it and approve. The installer waits.
3. The installer prints your remote MCP URL.

The box is then also reachable over your tailnet for SSH.
