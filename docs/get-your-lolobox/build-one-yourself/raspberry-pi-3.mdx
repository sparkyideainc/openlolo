## Parts

- Raspberry Pi 3
- Micro-USB power supply (5.1 V, 2.5 A)

## Steps

1. Flash Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit) to the microSD card with Raspberry Pi Imager. Turn on SSH in its settings.
2. Plug the iPhone into a USB port.
