## For every LoloBox

- An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Check **Settings → General → About** for the iOS version.
- A USB cable that can transfer data. A charge-only cable will not work.
- A power supply for your box.
- Your home Wi-Fi name and password.
- The AI app you want to connect.

The setup guide will explain when to turn on Developer Mode on your iPhone.

## If you are building your own

You also need:

- A [supported board](/docs/get-your-lolobox/build-one-yourself) and the parts in its build guide.
- A microSD memory card (16 GB or larger).
- A computer to prepare the memory card and run installation commands on the box.
- A free [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) account for the default remote access setup, used by AI apps such as Claude.ai and ChatGPT.
