## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Fit the heatsink

Fit the Radxa Heatsink 5519A over the RK3566 chip.
</Step>
<Step>
### Attach the PiSugar 3

It goes on the back of the board. Its pogo pins line up with the Radxa ZERO 3W.

<Callout type="warn">The PiSugar 3 charging chip reaches 50–60 °C at 2 A input. Do not touch the chip area while charging.</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
### Flash Armbian

Write Armbian (Debian 13) to the microSD card, then insert it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Plug in the iPhone

Use the **USB 3.0** port. The other USB-C port is the power input.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Checkpoint">Power it on. The green LED on the board double-blinks: Linux is running.</Callout>
