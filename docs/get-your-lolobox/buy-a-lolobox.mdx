## Already have your box?

If OpenLolo is already installed, you can skip the build and installation guides. Have your iPhone, a USB data cable and your home Wi-Fi password ready, then [turn on your LoloBox](/docs/pair-your-lolobox/turn-on-your-lolobox).

Keep any setup card or instructions that came with your box nearby. You will need the box's setup Wi-Fi name and password.

If your box does not have OpenLolo installed, follow the [installation guide](/docs/get-your-lolobox/build-one-yourself/first-boot) first.
